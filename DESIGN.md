# College Boy Cheesesteaks — design direction

A food-first, red-truck editorial system. The large condensed headlines borrow the directness of a food truck menu; the small uppercase utility labels behave like order tickets. The single deliberate visual flourish is the “Earn Your Degree” menu ladder, with the Doctorate image as its capstone. Everything else stays legible and useful.

Colors: ink `#161313`, truck red `#d31728`, deep red `#a40e1d`, paper `#f6f2ec`, white `#fff`, muted `#66615d`. Type: Impact/Arial Narrow for display, Arial/Helvetica for body and utility. The CSS custom properties in `dist/styles.css` are the runtime source for the palette and type roles.

The primary actions are Find the Truck and Order Pickup. A location is never invented; the Instagram link remains the fallback until the team maintains a confirmed schedule. The current repository uses branded graphic placeholders because the handoff did not include documented photography reuse approval. Approved photography may replace those placeholders only after its permission record is completed in `ASSET_MANIFEST.md`. Kevin's family photos and unapproved tribute text stay out of this preview. Preview pages have noindex metadata until client signoff and production migration.
